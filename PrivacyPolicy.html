RETIREMENT — PRIVACY POLICY Last updated 1 September 2026 THE SHORT VERSION Everything you type stays on this device. There is no account, no sign-in, and no server to send it to. We cannot see your numbers because they never leave your phone or browser. WHAT IS STORED Your age, retirement age, balance, contribution, salary, tax rate, spending plan, growth and inflation assumptions, and your theme choice. They are saved in this app's own local storage so you do not have to type them again. NO DATA COLLECTION No name, email address or phone number. No bank or brokerage connection. No analytics, advertising identifiers or crash reporting. Nothing is shared with anyone, because nothing is transmitted. DELETING IT Uninstalling the app removes everything. On the web, clearing this site's storage in your browser does the same. There is no copy anywhere else to request the deletion of. EDUCATION USE ONLY It is an educational calculator. It does not provide financial, investment, tax or legal advice, it is not a broker or adviser, and it has no relationship with any financial institution. Projections are arithmetic on the assumptions you enter, not predictions, and future returns are unknown. Talk to a qualified professional before acting on anything you see here. ALL AGES This app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them. Since nothing is transmitted to us, we hold no information about any user of any age.
